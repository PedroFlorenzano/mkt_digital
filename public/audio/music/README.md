# Background Music Tracks

Place royalty-free CC0 MP3 files here with these exact names:
- `energetic-upbeat.mp3`
- `smooth-corporate.mp3`
- `corporate-professional.mp3`
- `inspirational-rise.mp3`
- `upbeat-modern.mp3`

Requirements:
- Minimum 120 seconds duration each
- CC0 or equivalent royalty-free license
- Recommended source: https://freemusicarchive.org/ or https://pixabay.com/music/

Until these files are added, the video assembler will skip background music.
